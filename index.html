Privacy-by-Design

Data Security & DPDP Act Compliance

1. Zero Aadhaar & Biometric Storage

Sahayak never captures, stores, or transmits Aadhaar numbers, biometric fingerprints, or bank credentials. Citizens are verified directly on official government servers via external links.

2. Ephemeral In-Memory Computation

Citizen profiles are matched inside RAM using deterministic mathematical predicates and are completely purged unless the user explicitly logs in via OTP to save their plan.

3. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

Built strictly according to data minimization principles: minimal questionnaire fields, explicit citizen consent, and no commercial data monetization.

4. Official Domain Watermarking

Every application link is strictly anchored to verified .gov.in and .nic.in government origins to eliminate phishing, scam portals, and middleman fraud.

Operational Integrity

Risks & Mitigation Architecture

⚠️ Risk 1: Outdated Scheme Data & Changing Rules Government orders change income caps and deadlines frequently.

Mitigation: Automated weekly validation crawler combined with an Admin Review Layer stamping every scheme with an auditable "Last Verified" date and source gazette citation.

⚠️ Risk 2: AI Hallucinations in Eligibility An LLM could falsely promise benefits or hallucinate nonexistent criteria.

Mitigation: Zero LLM in decision making. 100% of matching is deterministic code (Boolean rules). The AI layer is strictly sandboxed for natural language phrasing and voice translation.

⚠️ Risk 3: Fraud, Phishing & Intermediary Exploitation Unscrupulous middlemen charge poor citizens bribes for free forms.

Mitigation: Strict domain whitelisting (only verified .gov.in domains), transparent display of official government fees (e.g. ₹30 for Income Certificate), and WhatsApp cards warning citizens against bribes.

⚠️ Risk 4: Privacy & Data Misuse Sensitive economic or social category data could be leaked.

Mitigation: Ephemeral in-memory matching; zero government beneficiary database scraping; compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023.
